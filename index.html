Official support, troubleshooting, and privacy information for the Lens Anatomy iOS app.

About Lens Anatomy

Lens Anatomy is an educational anatomy visualization app that uses the device camera to display interactive anatomical overlays of the hand, wrist, and forearm.

Educational use only. Lens Anatomy is not a medical device and should not be used for diagnosis, treatment, or medical decision-making.

Getting Started

  1. Open Lens Anatomy and allow camera access.
  2. Position your hand clearly in the camera view.
  3. Use good lighting for best results.
  4. Select Bones or Muscles.
  5. Use Align elbow when prompted.

Troubleshooting

Overlay not appearing correctly?

  • Move to a well-lit area.
  • Keep your full hand visible.
  • Avoid visually busy backgrounds.
  • Hold your hand steady briefly.
  • Move your phone farther away if part of your arm is outside the frame.

Camera access disabled?

Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera and enable access for Lens Anatomy.

Forearm overlay misaligned?

Use the Align elbow button and keep your wrist and forearm visible inside the camera frame.

Privacy

Lens Anatomy uses camera access to provide real-time anatomical visualizations.

Camera images are not stored or collected by the app.
