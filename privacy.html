Privacy Policy

Anatomy Lens

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Overview

Anatomy Lens is an educational anatomy visualization app designed to help users explore anatomical structures of the hand, wrist, and forearm.

We respect your privacy and have designed Anatomy Lens to minimize the collection of personal information.

Camera Access

Anatomy Lens uses your device's camera to provide real-time anatomical overlays.

Camera images are used for the purpose of generating the visualization and are not stored or collected by Anatomy Lens.

Information We Collect

Anatomy Lens does not collect personal information from users.

The app does not collect:

  • Names or contact information
  • Account information
  • Camera images or videos
  • Precise location information
  • Browsing history
  • Advertising identifiers

Artificial Intelligence Services

Anatomy Lens does not send user information, camera images, or other user data to OpenAI or other generative AI services.

Advertising and Tracking

Anatomy Lens does not display third-party advertising and does not use user data for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Third-Party Services

Anatomy Lens does not sell or share personal information with third parties for advertising purposes.

Children's Privacy

Anatomy Lens is an educational application and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Medical Disclaimer

Anatomy Lens is intended for educational purposes only. It is not a medical device and should not be used for diagnosis, treatment, or medical decision-making.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if Anatomy Lens adds or changes features that affect user privacy. Updates will be posted on this page.
